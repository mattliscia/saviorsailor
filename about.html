About Savior Sailor

Hello! Welcome to Savior Sailor! My name is Alex, but I used to go by FCA2(SW) Velichko during my 8 years of service in the United States Navy. With over 22 months at sea, I often felt something was missing. This led me to found “Savior Sailor” and create a resource for service members and their loved ones.

At Savior Sailor, we specialize in care packages designed to bring comfort and a touch of home to sailors while they are away. These packages are carefully curated based on my experiences and insights gathered from over 30 fellow service members. Each package is filled with items that make the transition from land to sea smoother, whether it’s your first deployment or your last.

Our mission is to ensure that service members feel prepared and supported. Thank you so much for your service to this great country and for your support. I look forward to connecting with you in the future!
