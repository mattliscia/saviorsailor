Snacks and Drinks to Pack for Deployment

The galley feeds you, but it feeds you on its schedule, and the ship's store sells out of the good stuff in the first two weeks. This is the list of what to stock in your locker. These are some ideas to get you started, but make sure you buy your favorites.

FCA2(SW) Alex Velichko

Founder, Savior Sailor · 8+ years U.S. Navy, 22+ months at sea

AeroPress

More compact than a French press AND makes a smoother Cup of Joe!

Real coffee underway is a luxury very few people have. The AeroPress is plastic, nearly unbreakable and makes a better cup than anything on the ship.

25

Spices and Sauces

Taco Tuesday and they ran out of hot sauce?!? Good thing you have your own. Burger Wednesday and there is no more ketchup?!? WELL…..good thing you have your own. You get the point haha make sure to get spices that you use regularly as well.

Galley food is cooked for hundreds and seasoned for none of them. A small kit of your own spices and a bottle of hot sauce fixes almost any meal.
