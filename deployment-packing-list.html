Why I Wrote This List

Hello! I am so glad we have found each other. My name is Alex, but I used to go by FCA2(SW) Velichko. Over 8 years of service in the United States Navy and more than 22 months collectively at sea, I always seemed to be missing something. This is why I founded Savior Sailor and created this resource.

I personally made a list and then interviewed 30+ service members, and with some time the Savior Sailor ebook was the outcome. Here it is as a set of web pages so you can read it on your phone, share a chapter with a family member, or check things off while you shop. Whether it is your first or last time leaving home, Savior Sailor is here for you.
