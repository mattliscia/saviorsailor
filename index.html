About Savior Sailor

Over 8 years of service in the United States Navy and more than 22 months collectively at sea, I always seemed to be missing something. This is why I founded “Savior Sailor” and have created this resource. In my eBook you will find all the items needed for a smooth transition from land to sea. Whether it is your first OR last time leaving home, Savior Sailor is here for you!

Alex
