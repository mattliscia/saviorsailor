Our Story

Born from Experience, Built with Heart

Over 8 years of service in the United States Navy and more than 22 months collectively at sea, I always seemed to be missing something. That's why I founded Savior Sailor.

Whether it's your first deployment or your last time leaving home, Savior Sailor is here for you. Our care packages are thoughtfully curated by veterans who understand what sailors truly need.

FCA2(SW) Alex Velichko

Founder, Savior Sailor
