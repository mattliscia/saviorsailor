Sponsor a Sailor

Whether you're a company or a individual patriot, join us in supporting our sailors by sponsoring a sailor in need. You can choose to sponsor a sailor from your hometown or one at random. Your sponsorship will provide essential items and heartfelt messages to make their time at sea more comfortable and enjoyable.

Why Sponsor a Sailor?

  • Support Our Troops

    Show your appreciation and support for the brave men and women serving our country by providing them with essential items and comfort.

  • Make a Difference

    Your sponsorship helps to boost the morale of sailors, letting them know they are valued and remembered while they are away from home.

  • Personal Connection

    Establish a personal connection with a sailor by sending them a message of encouragement and support along with your sponsored package.
