Support those who Serve

Take part in our Sponsor a Sailor initiative to make a difference. Send essential care packages to sailors who could use your support.

Your contribution not only provides much-needed supplies but also boosts morale and shows appreciation for their service.

How It Works

Start with a meeting with Alex, who will assist in matching your business with sailors in need or from your local area.

You can choose to sponsor multiple sailors, support a new sailor each month, or opt for a one-time sponsorship. Alex will tailor the plan to fit your goals, ensuring a meaningful and impactful experience.

Showcase Your Support

Receive pictures or plaques of the sailors you sponsor to display in your establishment. This not only showcases your commitment to supporting the military but also highlights your business’s dedication to the community.
