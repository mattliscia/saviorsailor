Over-the-Counter Medicine to Pack for Deployment

General over-the-counter medicine is very handy to have in case medical has a long line, or you need something at an odd hour and don't want to deal with waking people up at night. Keep it in its original packaging, follow the label, and check your command's policy on what you can bring aboard.

FCA2(SW) Alex Velichko

Founder, Savior Sailor · 8+ years U.S. Navy, 22+ months at sea
